## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### ❓ What is IPTV Pro for Mac?

📺 IPTV Pro is an IPTV playback and playlist interface for users who want to access compatible IPTV streams on a Mac.

### ❓ Can IPTV Pro play live TV?

📡 It can play compatible live IPTV streams supplied through supported and authorized IPTV sources.

### ❓ Does IPTV Pro provide TV channels by itself?

🔗 IPTV players generally depend on the playlist or IPTV service supplied by the user. Channel availability depends on that source.

### ❓ Can I use an M3U playlist?

📋 If the Mac version supports the relevant M3U format, a compatible playlist can be used as an IPTV source.

### ❓ Does IPTV Pro support EPG?

🕒 Supported EPG functionality depends on the application version and the guide information supplied with your IPTV source.

### ❓ Can I create a favorites list?

⭐ Supported favorites functionality can make frequently watched channels easier to access.

### ❓ Can I watch IPTV in full screen?

🖥️ Full-screen viewing is available where supported by the application and playback environment.

### ❓ Does IPTV Pro require internet access?

🌐 Yes. Online IPTV streams normally require an active internet connection.

### ❓ Why does an IPTV stream buffer?

📡 Buffering can be caused by internet speed, network congestion, provider-side limitations, server load, or the quality of the stream source.

### ❓ Is IPTV Pro itself a TV service?

📺 No. An IPTV player should be treated as the playback/interface layer. You need an appropriate IPTV source or service for channels and programming.
